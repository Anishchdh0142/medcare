About MEDCARE

MEDCARE is an advanced, corporative-clinical healthcare platform that bridges the gap between patients, pharmacists, and doctors using cutting-edge AI and clinical data. It is designed to provide authoritative and stable healthcare management for hospitals and diagnostic centers.

Aim of MEDCARE

Our primary aim is to optimize treatment safety and prevent dangerous drug interactions by delivering accessible, FDA-verified pharmacist expertise on demand. We strive to create a seamless ecosystem that prioritizes patient health, lowers costs, and improves clinical efficiency.

Pill Identifier

Describe the pill and we'll show you what matches — then you confirm against the picture. If it can't be identified safely, we say so.

Turn the pill over and read both sides. The markings matter more than the colour or the shape — they are what tell two identical-looking tablets apart.

Many pills are marked on one side only. A score line — the groove for splitting — is not a marking.

Any one of these helps. You don't need all four — but the more you give, the shorter the list.

This tool matches markings against FDA labelling data. It is not a substitute for a pharmacist, and a description cannot rule out a counterfeit. If a pill did not come from a pharmacy, do not take it. In an emergency call 911, or Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222.
